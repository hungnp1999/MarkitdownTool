# MarkItDownTool Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

MarkItDownTool is an independent open-source project maintained by:

**Nguyễn Phú Hùng**

Repository:

https://github.com/hungnp1999/MarkitdownTool

This project aims to create a welcoming, respectful, and productive environment for users, contributors, reviewers, and community members.

We are committed to providing a community experience that is:

- Friendly
- Professional
- Inclusive
- Collaborative
- Respectful of different backgrounds and perspectives

Participation in this community should be free from harassment, discrimination, hostility, or personal attacks.

---

## Community Goals

The purpose of this community is to:

- Improve MarkItDownTool
- Share knowledge
- Report issues
- Suggest new features
- Discuss document processing, OCR, Markdown workflows, and related technologies
- Help new users and contributors

All participants are encouraged to contribute constructively.

---

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive community include:

- Being respectful and courteous.
- Providing constructive feedback.
- Helping others solve problems.
- Sharing ideas openly and professionally.
- Respecting different opinions and experiences.
- Reporting bugs responsibly.
- Discussing technical decisions using facts and evidence.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable in any project space, including GitHub Issues, Discussions, Pull Requests, Releases, or external project communication channels.

### Harassment and Abuse

- Personal attacks.
- Insults.
- Threats.
- Intimidation.
- Hate speech.
- Harassment of any kind.

### Disruptive Conduct

- Trolling.
- Deliberately provoking arguments.
- Repeated off-topic discussions.
- Repeated spam or self-promotion.
- Excessive hostility toward contributors or maintainers.

### Privacy Violations

- Sharing private information without consent.
- Publishing personal contact information.
- Doxxing or exposing personal details.

### Malicious Activities

- Submitting malicious code.
- Attempting to compromise project security.
- Encouraging unlawful activities.
- Sharing malware or harmful content.

### Intellectual Property Violations

- Submitting content that infringes copyright.
- Removing or altering attribution notices.
- Misrepresenting authorship of another person's work.
- Claiming ownership of contributions created by other individuals.

---

## Respect for Maintainers

MarkItDownTool is currently maintained by a single independent developer.

Community members are expected to understand that:

- Feature requests may not be implemented immediately.
- Pull requests may not be accepted.
- Issue responses may be delayed.
- Technical decisions ultimately remain the responsibility of the maintainer.

Disagreement is acceptable.

Disrespect is not.

---

## Contributions and Ownership

Contributors retain ownership of their original contributions.

By submitting contributions to this project (including pull requests, code snippets, documentation, and other materials), contributors agree that their submissions may be reviewed, modified, accepted, rejected, or incorporated into the project.

Contributors should only submit work that they have the legal right to share.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reserves the right to take appropriate action to protect the project and community.

Possible actions include:

### 1. Informal Reminder

Used for minor or first-time violations.

### 2. Warning

A formal warning regarding inappropriate behavior.

### 3. Content Removal

Removal or modification of comments, discussions, issues, pull requests, or other content that violates this Code of Conduct.

### 4. Temporary Restriction

Temporary limitation of participation in project activities.

### 5. Permanent Ban

Permanent removal from participation in project spaces.

The severity of enforcement depends on the nature and impact of the violation.
