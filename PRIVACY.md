# Privacy Notice

## Overview

MarkItDownTool is designed to perform document processing locally on the user's device.

The software is intended to operate without transmitting user documents
to external servers operated by the author.

---

## Data Collection

MarkItDownTool does not intentionally collect:

- Personal information
- Account information
- Contact information
- Device identifiers
- Document contents

The author does not receive copies of files processed by the software.

---

## Local Processing

OCR processing and Markdown conversion are performed locally on the user's computer.

Generated files remain under the user's control unless the user independently chooses to share them.

---

## Third-Party Components

Some third-party components used by the software may have their own privacy policies, license terms, and operational behaviors.

Users are responsible for reviewing and understanding those terms when using such components.

---

## Future Changes

Future versions of the software may introduce optional features that connect to external services.

If this occurs, the privacy documentation will be updated accordingly.
